# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy and never post customer data, credentials, or private communications.
- Accept correction and help keep discussions useful to Magento users and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, and knowingly sharing unsafe or malicious code are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the [Haroone Agency contact form](https://haroone.com/contact). Include the repository name and enough context for the maintainers to review the incident. Do not include unrelated personal or production data.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing this project or its community.
